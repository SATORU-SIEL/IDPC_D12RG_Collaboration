The main result is that the topology-dependent stability boundary reproduced, but in a sharper form than the B7.5c summary.

C12 remained reverse-stable.
C13 remained full-self-consistent stable.
C10 remained near the boundary.
C14 did not remain a boundary case in this run; it moved to the full-self-consistent side.

Across the C8-C16 sweep, the pattern was:

- reverse-stable: C8, C12
- boundary / transition: C9, C10, C11, C15
- full-self-consistent stable: C13, C14, C16

So the result should not be read as a single global winner between reverse-only C -> AB and full self-consistent AB <-> C.

The stronger reading is that C12-neighborhood topology controls which representation couples best to the readout.

C12 is on the reverse-side readout branch.
C13, C14, and C16 move to the full-self-consistent branch.
C9, C10, C11, and C15 occupy transition or boundary positions.

This strengthens the B7.5c interpretation: the boundary itself is becoming more reproducible than any one representation arm.

The perturbation results also matter. C12 perturbations mostly preserved the reverse-side pattern, while C13 perturbations mostly preserved the full-self-consistent pattern. C14 was more mixed, which makes it useful as a transition probe rather than a clean fixed point.

fractions as diagnostics first, explanations later.

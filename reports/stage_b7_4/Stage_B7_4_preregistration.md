# Stage B7.4 Preregistration: Vacuum-layer C as Quadrature Regulation Between Two Phi12 Systems

Status: preregistered before the formal Stage B7.4 execution.

## Motivation

Stage B6/B7 narrowed the viable C structure through a failure ladder:

- objectification failed;
- direct A-B failed;
- scalar / broad C failed;
- receiver / standpoint-sensitive C survived.

Stage B7.3/B7.3a then reconnected the surviving C to C12 topology. The surviving structure was not a broad or substitutable C, but a narrower receiver/magnitude/standpoint-sensitive C.

Luke's updated mathematical suggestion changes the B7.4 target. The target is not an exact Phi24 topology. It is a pair of Phi12 / C12-like ring systems, each preserving factor-of-12 structure, while the phase relation between the two systems is held at precisely 90 degrees, independently of frequency or magnitude.

Here, the vacuum is not emptiness. It is a regulated balance state: two factor-of-12 systems remain distinct, not collapsed into one Phi24 object, while their relation is held in exact quadrature. In this framing, Vacuum-layer C is not either ring alone, but the invariant balance between the two rings.

Following Marcel's caution, the Vacuum-layer language is treated strictly as hypothesis motivation, not as an empirical conclusion.

## Primary Question

Does a Phi-weighted or differentiation/integration-invariant C representation improve C12 / quadrature topology-specific readout beyond the B7 receiver/magnitude/standpoint candidates?

## Updated Hypothesis

The C that survived in B7 may reconnect to C12 not as a single C12 readout, and not as a Phi24 topology, but as a regulated quadrature relation between two factor-of-12 phase systems. This regulated 90-degree balance may be the operational form of Vacuum-layer C.

## Prescreen Boundary

A short local prescreen was used only to orient this audit design. It carries no evidential weight for the formal claim. The preregistered Stage B7.4 execution decides the result.

The prescreen orientation was approximately:

- architecture only: weak;
- 90-degree event offset only: weak;
- 90-degree regulatory constraint: signal appeared;
- architecture + 90-degree regulation: signal also appeared;
- regulation-only 90-degree control: also strong, sometimes comparable or stronger.

This motivates explicitly separating architecture-only, regulation-only, and architecture-plus-regulation arms.

Additional diagnostic candidates may be retained for tables but are not primary decision candidates.

Primary representation controls:

The central representation question is whether `phi_differential_invariant_c` adds something genuinely new, or collapses back into `receiver_only_c` under another basis.

## Topology and Regulation Arms

The formal audit must separate:

- single C12;
- two separate C12 / Phi12-like rings;
- paired rings with 0, 45, 90, and 180 degree phase offsets;
- paired rings with explicit 90-degree regulatory enforcement;
- regulation-only 90-degree control;
- architecture + regulation;
- broken factor-of-12 controls;
- reversed second-ring controls;
- H24 / Phi24-style controls;
- no-topology, reversed C12, side-broken C12, shuffled C12, and neighboring C10/C11/C13/C14 controls.

## Event Controls

The audit must include:

- endogenous event schedule;
- time-shifted event schedule;
- random event schedule.

## Decision Rules

Primary positive support requires effect-size separation, not only binary pass/fail.

For the Phi-weighted / invariant claim to be supported:

1. `phi_eigen_energy_c` or `phi_differential_invariant_c` must improve the target readout beyond `receiver_only_c` and `receiver_standpoint_magnitude_c`.
2. The improvement must be visible in C12 / quadrature-specific topology arms, not only in topology-agnostic or regulation-only arms.
3. The target topology/regulation arm must exceed no-topology, reversed, side-broken, shuffled, neighboring-cycle, time-shifted, and random-event controls.
4. `phi_differential_invariant_c` must show nontrivial separation from `receiver_only_c`, including both effect-size separation and feature-correlation diagnostics.

If the Phi-weighted / invariant candidates do not improve beyond the B7 receiver/magnitude/standpoint candidates, the safer boundary remains the B7.3 receiver/magnitude/standpoint interpretation.

If the quadrature-regulation arms are strong but regulation-only is equal or stronger than architecture + regulation, the conclusion must not claim that paired C12 architecture is required. The narrower conclusion would be that 90-degree regulatory balance is the active factor under this operationalization.

Required caution:

This can be stated only if the preregistered effect-size and control criteria are met. Otherwise, Vacuum-layer C remains hypothesis motivation only.
